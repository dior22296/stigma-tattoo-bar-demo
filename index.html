About Stigma Tattoo Bar

A tattoo shop and a bar under one roof, right on Orange Ave.

Stigma Tattoo Bar pairs a working tattoo studio with a full bar in Downtown Orlando. Eight artists cover the chairs, so whether you booked a custom piece weeks out or walked in on a whim, there is an artist who fits your idea.

One booking flow covers the whole shop: call once, talk through your piece, and the team will match you with the right artist, walk you through the consult and deposit, and get you on the books.
